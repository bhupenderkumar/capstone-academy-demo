Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-K program provides a nurturing and engaging environment for young learners, fostering social, emotional, and cognitive development through play-based activities and hands-on projects.

📚

Elementary

In our Elementary program, students build a strong foundation in reading, writing, mathematics, and science, while exploring their interests and passions through arts and physical education.

🤔

Middle/Upper

In Middle School and Upper School, students engage in rigorous academic courses, develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills, and explore their interests through electives and extracurricular activities.

💻

STEM

Our STEM program provides students with a hands-on, project-based learning experience, exploring the intersection of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics to solve real-world problems.

🎨

Arts

Our Arts program offers students the opportunity to express themselves through various mediums, including music, dance, theater, and visual arts, fostering creativity, self-expression, and appreciation for the arts.

🏈

Athletics

Our Athletics program provides students with the opportunity to develop physical skills, teamwork, and sportsmanship, while competing in local and regional leagues.
